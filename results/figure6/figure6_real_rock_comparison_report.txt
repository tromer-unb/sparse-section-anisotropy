INTERPRETATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Spatial replication reduces tensor reconstruction error and position
sensitivity in both lithologies. The carbonate remains more difficult,
consistent with its much stronger spatial heterogeneity and higher
reference-model residual.
The 1.706% p95 reference number is a Bentheimer calibration of the frozen
50k x 5-seed protocol; it is not presented as an independent EdB-1
uncertainty estimate.
